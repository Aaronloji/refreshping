RefreshPing Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

RefreshPing is a Chrome extension published by Stackbit (Aaron Lopez, Costa Rica). This policy explains what data the extension handles.

What stays on your device

Your refresh settings (interval, text to watch, active hours, sound option) and the list of sites where you used them are saved with Chrome's storage on your computer. They are not sent to us or to anyone else.

When you set a text alert, the extension reads the visible text of that page only to check whether your text appears or disappears. This check runs inside your browser. Page content is never stored or sent anywhere.

What is sent over the internet

Only if you buy RefreshPing Pro: when you activate, validate or remove your license key, the extension sends the key and an activation label to Lemon Squeezy (api.lemonsqueezy.com), the store that processes the payment. Lemon Squeezy's privacy policy applies to your purchase.

The free version makes no network requests of its own.

What we do not do

No analytics, no tracking, no ads, no browsing history collection. We do not sell or share user data.

Permissions

Access to a website is requested only when you set a text alert on it, and only for that website. You can remove it at any time in Chrome's extension settings.
